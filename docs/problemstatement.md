### Problem Statement  
At the University of Eswatini, students often enter exam halls without proper identity checks, and lecturers only 
verify IDs while collecting exam cards during the exam. Exam scripts are collected either at the end of the session or 
when the student indicates that they have finished writing, but the process is manual and prone to mistakes. This lack 
of consistent verification and tracking creates vulnerabilities that make it easy for exam cards or scripts to go missing 
exam cards and scripts to be mismatched, and for impersonation to occur. Without a secure, consolidated identity token 
and a reliable method of linking scripts to verified students, the university cannot ensure transparency, accountability, 
or trust in its examination process.
